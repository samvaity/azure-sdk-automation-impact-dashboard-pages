What can be learned

Workload distribution

Assignments and open work show whether review demand is concentrated and where balancing or additional coverage may help.

Waiting patterns

Assignment-to-signoff timing can identify review paths worth examining, but it includes service-team responses, PR readiness, and review complexity.

Automation opportunities

Repeatedly slow paths can be inspected for missing context, manual coordination, or routine checks that automation could prepare earlier.

Evidence limits

GitHub events measure workflow elapsed time, not cognitive effort. Raw events and pull-request identifiers remain outside the public site.
